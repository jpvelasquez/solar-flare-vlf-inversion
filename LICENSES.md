## SAVNET / Open VLF data

The file

`data/savnet/PLO-20080325.fits`

contains VLF observations from the **South America VLF Network (SAVNET)**, operated by CRAAM (Mackenzie Radio Astronomy and Astrophysics Center).

SAVNET data are publicly available through **Open VLF: Scientific Open Data Initiative for CRAAM's SAVNET and AWESOME VLF Data**. The official Registry of Open Data on AWS entry states:

> There are no restrictions on the use of this data.

For reproducibility, this repository includes only the SAVNET record corresponding to the event analyzed in the study, **25 March 2008**.

Source and citation information:

- Open VLF registry entry: https://registry.opendata.aws/craam-open-vlf/
- Open VLF project: https://open-vlf.web.app/

The SAVNET observational data are **not covered by the MIT License** in this repository; they retain their original provenance and data-use terms.

## GOES / NOAA data

The GOES-10 X-Ray Sensor data included under `data/goes/` originate from the **NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information (NOAA/NCEI)**. The archived file is the external source product used to characterize the solar X-ray forcing in the study.

Source:

https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/data/goes-space-environment-monitor/access/science/xrs/goes10/xrsf-l2-avg1m_science/

These NOAA source data retain their original provenance and are **not covered by the MIT License** applied to the project's original software.
